08Business impact
The business case
The planner starts from a ready offer, not a blank lead.

For the installer

  • Each lead arrives with a design, a price range and a draft message
  • The planner handles exceptions; every edit re-derives the numbers
  • Nothing is sent until the planner approves
  • White-label: customers see the installer's brand

For the homeowner

  • An answer today, not a callback
  • A reason for every number, one tap away
  • Prices marked estimate until the installer confirms
  • Nothing sold to them; they choose to hand over
