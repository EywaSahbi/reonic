# experience/ — reo's voice, flow and look

> Back to the [project README](../README.md). Owner: `RaneemK-commits`.

reo's voice belongs to the product side, not to the backend. The reo backend **loads these files at runtime**, so the voice can change without touching code.

| File | What it defines |
|---|---|
| [persona.md](persona.md) | who reo is (Sonnfeld's energy advisor, white-label), tone, how to say numbers, what reo may and may not say or promise |
| [flow.md](flow.md) | the conversation stages, which fact is asked in which order, the handoff moment, what the installer sees |
| [brand.md](brand.md) | the visual brand tokens (colours, type, spacing) the UI follows |

Rules that matter: every number reo says comes from the engine in the same turn; customer prices are estimates until the installer confirms; reo never promises a grant, a date or a guarantee.
